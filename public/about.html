Bagsy

About

Bagsy started as one person's ad-hoc habit of prompting an AI chatbot to help plan trips — and the realization that a proper travel-planning tool should do the same research, but reliably and tailored to whoever's actually asking, not just whoever built it.

It's a small, personal, non-commercial project — not a company, not a startup, just one person's side project currently in public beta. There's no team, no funding, no business model. If something breaks or feels half-finished, that's because it might genuinely be — that's what a beta is for.

A few things Bagsy tries to do differently from just asking a chatbot directly:

  • Suggestions are personalized to your stored travel profile, not a fixed checklist — a parent needing strollers and someone wanting nightlife get genuinely different criteria and destinations.
  • Specific, checkable claims (accessibility, flight routes, typical weather) come from live web search where possible, not just the model's training data guessing confidently.
  • No login. No account. No shared data between visitors — everything's scoped to an anonymous, signed session cookie the moment you first arrive.

It's actively being improved based on real feedback — see the 💬 Feedback button on the main app, which goes straight to whoever's running this. If you'd like to know more about what data is collected, see the Privacy notice; usage terms are on the Terms page.
